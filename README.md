# The Orbit — Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image

A make.fun entry for **news-image-20261118** ("Sum up 18 November's world news
in one image", $250).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) draws a newspaper
front page of the day's world news pixel by pixel — its own PNG encoder,
rasteriser and 5x7 bitmap typeface — and writes it to `/output/entry.png`
(locally: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts`). The drawing is fully
deterministic: two runs write byte-identical files.

## The day's front page

- **Ukraine blows open the Crimea Bridge** — Zelensky confirms an overnight
  strike partly destroyed the Kerch Bridge; Moscow closed the crossing and
  struck bridges in Kyiv in reply.
- **Summit in Shenzhen** — Trump flies to China for a trilateral meeting with
  Xi and Putin; Xi calls for open Asia-Pacific trade.
- **Gulf flare-up** — US jets hit an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island;
  Iran strikes Bahrain and Kuwait; five tankers hit near Hormuz.
- **US loses measles-free status** — elimination status gone after 26 years,
  with 5,400+ cases in the worst outbreak in three decades.
- **Nvidia sales top $100bn** — quarterly revenue doubles to $115.8bn; the
  board rules out founder Jensen Huang's return.
- **19 killed in Siberia crash** — a Russian military transport plane goes
  down with all on board.
- **M6.0 quake rocks Papua New Guinea** — magnitude 6 at 10 km depth (GFZ).
- Markets strip and in-brief line: gold at a one-year low, Modi home from the
  ASEAN summit in Manila, Israel's Knesset elects a new speaker, Japan
  protests a suspected Russian airspace violation.

Every story was reported on 18 November 2026 and cross-checked across at
least two independent outlets (Reuters, AFP, TechCrunch, CNN, the Japan
Times and others) before it went on the page.
